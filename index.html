About Us

Alt Tribe is a marketplace for the wonderfully different.

We bring together 100+ independent brands across jewellery, clothing, home & decor, skincare, wellness, bags and edibles — working with small businesses and emerging labels who want real-world retail without building an entire store from scratch.

But Alt Tribe is more than a place to sell. It's an ecosystem of 100+ brands under one roof, creating a constantly changing world of discovery — homegrown brands, independent creators, sustainable choices, unexpected finds.

Basically, if you're tired of the same old, same old, you're probably going to like it here.
